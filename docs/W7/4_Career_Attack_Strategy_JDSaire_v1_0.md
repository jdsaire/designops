## 5. The McKinsey track — gap acknowledged, roadmap proposed

**Honest read:** you hold real McKinsey-adjacent signals — Forward graduate, Tricontinental MBA 1st in class with a Strategic Consulting specialization, teaching at scale, an engineering spine. What you don't yet hold: consulting delivery experience, case-interview fluency under pressure, a brand-name corporate seat, and an internal advocate. McKinsey hires people like you most readily as **experienced/specialist hires into McKinsey Digital, McKinsey Design, or Implementation** — not through the campus generalist funnel. That's good news: the specialist door values exactly the hybrid you've built, and the Tier-1 seat you're pursuing *is* the preparation.

**Short term (now–6 months) — land the Tier-1 seat; it is the McKinsey prerequisite, not a detour:**
- Everything in §3–4. A BCP/BBVA seat gives you the brand-name delivery record, the transformation vocabulary, and the salary floor to prepare without pressure.
- Activate the McKinsey Forward alumni community now (one event or one directory conversation per month) — lowest-cost McKinsey proximity available to you today.
- Start light case exposure: one casebook case per week as familiarization, not drilling. (Claude stack: a dedicated **"Case Sparring" project** where Fable 5 runs interviewer-led cases — profitability, market entry, digital transformation — and scores structure, math, and synthesis per round.)

**Medium term (6–24 months) — build the experienced-hire dossier:**
- 6–12 months of measurable impact in the seat, framed McKinsey-style from day one: baseline → intervention → quantified delta. (Log these monthly in a private impact file; Fable maintains it.)
- Case fluency to interview standard: 2–3 timed mocks/week for the 8–10 weeks before applying; add Solve-game-style drills. Claude runs the mocks and the post-mortems; a human practice partner joins in the final month — Forward alumni are the natural pool.
- Target the **specialist tracks by name** — McKinsey Digital / Design (Product, DesignOps-adjacent) and Implementation — via referral from the Forward network, not a cold portal application.
- Keep the weekly content running: a public, rigorous body of thinking is unusual among applicants and consistent with how the firm evaluates "distinctive" candidates.
- Honest checkpoint at month 12: if the seat is compounding and the case scores aren't, the seat wins — McKinsey remains a door, not the identity. The identity is already built.

## 7. Anti-demotivation rules (binding)

1. **One sitting or it's mis-scoped.** Split or cut; never carry a half-open epic into the pipeline.
2. **Rejection is pipeline data, not verdict.** Log it, note the stage it died at, move the next row forward. Three data points before any strategy change.
3. **Presence beats perfection.** A good post shipped Monday outranks a perfect post shipped never; the checklist is the quality floor, not a gate for polish.
4. **No new artifacts.** Execution is over. If an action requires building something new, it's out of scope until an interviewer asks for it.
5. **Weekly wins are counted, out loud, every Friday.** The review always starts with what shipped — the pipeline runs on evidence of motion, exactly like everything else you build.
