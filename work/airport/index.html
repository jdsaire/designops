Charging what was never charged

Lima's airport had never charged international transfer passengers a fee. I designed the web channel to collect it, one of four ways to pay, and handed it over.

Airport Operations Customer Experience UX Research UX Design Service Design
Roles — Internal consultant · UX/UI designer · Planning coordinator Duration — May – Oct 2025 · Lima Airport Partners Scope — Planning → UX/UI design → prototyping → evaluation → handoff
Open the live TUUA platform

A passenger lands in Lima and connects to another international flight. At the new terminal they owe a fee the airport had never charged, and no channel existed to collect it. Five central areas each held a piece of the problem. As the internal consultant from Processes and Innovation, I coordinated the planning between them, designed the web payment flow, evaluated it and handed it to the team that built it. TUUA Transfer runs today, charging 130,000+ international transfer passengers a month.

Act 06 · The Contrast

The platform is live, it isn't mine, and the gap is public.

Public, unauthenticated pages only. No transaction was submitted, no session was captured, and no data was collected beyond what any visitor sees.
