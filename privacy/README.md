# privacy/

The privacy policy, served at `/privacy/`: who is responsible, what the two forms collect, who processes it, transfers, retention, rights, cookies and changes. It isn't in the navigation; the footer row on every page links it.

| File | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| [`index.html`](index.html) | Anyone changing the page's markup. It loads its script from [`../assets/js/pages/privacy/`](../assets/js/pages/privacy/README.md) and its styles from [`../assets/css/pages/privacy/`](../assets/css/pages/privacy/README.md) |
| [`i18n/privacy.en.json`](i18n/privacy.en.json), [`i18n/privacy.es.json`](i18n/privacy.es.json) | Anyone changing the policy's text. Same keys in both languages, in page order. The date and the forms' field lists inside it are filled from the forms' own labels when the policy changes |
